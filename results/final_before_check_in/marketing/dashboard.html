Can you build me a dashboard on how our marketing channels are doing?

Which marketing channels bring customers who place a second completed order within 12 months of their first?

12-month repeat rate, September 2025 first-time buyers (all channels)
n/a
Share of customers whose first completed order was in Sep 2025 and who ordered again within 12 months.

Findings

  • Search and Organic bring customers who return most reliably: for Sep 2025 first-time buyers, Organic had a 17.4% repeat rate (69 customers) and Search 11.4% (386 customers).
  • Across all channels, the 12-month repeat rate rose from 4.0% for Oct 2023 first-time buyers to 11.8% for Sep 2025 first-time buyers.
  • Search is the biggest source of new customers, with 386 of the 544 first-time buyers in Sep 2025, so it drives most of the repeat customers (44 of 64).
  • Display, Email and Facebook groups are usually under 30 customers a month, so their rates swing a lot, for example Display went from 0.0% in Apr 2025 to 25.0% in Mar 2025; no firm ranking of these channels is possible from single months.
  • Channel is the signup channel on the customer record, and a customer counts as repeat only with a second completed order. Recent cohorts may be slightly understated because some recent orders may still become complete.
  • The data has no marketing spend, so it cannot show which channel gives the best value for money. Budget moves should also use spend figures from outside this data.

Data notes

Four problems could distort the repeat rate. These are future-dated orders, duplicate order IDs, orders with no customer, and 999-item style orders. Date-order errors need a quick decision. Duplicate emails may mean duplicate customer accounts, and that needs a decision because it affects who counts as a repeat customer. The two spelling flags are false alarms, and neither field is used in this brief.

Period: 2023-10-01 to 2025-09-30.

Decisions assumed:

  • Same email = same person? Recommended: No. Treat each user id as one customer and do not merge accounts. The dashboard shows how many customers share an email as a caveat. If you tell us the same email is the same person, we will merge accounts, use the earliest completed order across them and the channel of the earliest account.
  • Window 'last 2 years': first orders from 2023-10-01 to 2025-09-30 (24 cohorts), the latest cohorts with a full 12 months of follow-up.
  • 12-month rule: 12 calendar months from the first order's timestamp (first created_at + 12 months), not 365 days. Needs your sign-off.
  • Second purchase: a different Complete order_id with a strictly later created_at than the first. Items in the same order do not count. Orders with the same timestamp do not count.
  • Channel is the single signup channel stored on the customer record (users.traffic_source), labelled 'Signup channel'. It may not be the channel that led to the first purchase.
  • Small groups: cohorts under 30 customers are flagged but still shown. A pooled quarterly view and a 24-month total per channel are offered, built from added-up counts. The threshold of 30 is our suggestion.
  • Audience assumed to be the marketing lead or budget owner.
  • Order status is the current status. Recent Shipped or Processing orders may still turn Complete, so the latest cohorts may be slightly understated.
  • Future-dated orders (2027-2028), orders with no customer and duplicate order IDs are removed as per the audit's proposed rules. The removed counts are shown on the dashboard.
  • Spend and value for money by channel cannot be shown. The data has no marketing spend. Organic is also not a paid channel.
