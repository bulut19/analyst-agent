# Check-in before building

**Q:** How many items should a brand or category need before it can be ranked? We expect very few of the 2,756 brands to reach 300 items in 12 months. Options: 300 items for both categories and brands; 300 for categories, 100 for brands; 300 for categories, and for brands the 50 with the most items sold. I'd suggest: 300 items for both categories and brands, unless you prefer otherwise. (A higher cut-off may leave only a handful of brands on the ranking, and a lower one adds more brands but with noisier rates.)

**A:** 300 for both is fine. That's in line with what I said, and I'd rather see a short list of brands that really matter than a long list of noisy ones.

**Q:** Some categories overlap: Socks and Socks & Hosiery, Pants and Pants & Capris, Suits and Suits & Sport Coats. Should we combine each pair? Options: Keep them separate, as they are in the data; Combine the three obvious pairs; Let merchandising give us the mapping first. I'd suggest: Keep them separate, as they are in the data, unless you prefer otherwise. (Combining adds their volume together, which can lift a category over the 300 cut-off and change its rank and rate.)

**A:** Keep them separate as they are in the data for now. I don't want to guess at groupings before a supplier conversation, and I'll check with merchandising if one of them comes up as a candidate.
